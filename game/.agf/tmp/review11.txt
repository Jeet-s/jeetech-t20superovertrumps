FAIL level_design: level-design review 7/10 (bar 8), 0 level(s) below 6/10: A deep, cleverly designed trump-card ladder with unusually clear teaching intent and a sound introduce-alone-then-combine structure. It is held back by a hard late difficulty ramp (mid-skill win rates of 55-60% in Ch5-6), deterministic solved-puzzle feel, repeated 'find the unbeatable stat' loops, thin breathers late on, and a truncated finale. It is strong but not yet at Marvel Snap or FC Mobile polish. Curve: Strong, well-structured teach→practise→combine→boss rhythm across 6 chapters, with each mechanic introduced alone (aggressive L11, safe L13, free hit L21, review L24, powerplay L27, weights L31-33, captain L34, fog L41, wildcard L43, mega pot L45) and a boss closing each chapter. Problems: (1) Difficulty runs too hot too early in places. Mid-skill win rates fall from ~100% (L1) to 55-69% by L20-L30 and 55-60% in Ch5-6, while the design doc promises a 2-minute fun fix. Late-chapter casual win rates of 54-65% with stars gated behind perfect play will feel punishing for the 13-35 casual audience. (2) Heavy reliance on the same recurring cards and same 'find the unbeatable stat, count the rival out' solution. That makes many units feel like one puzzle in different clothes (L2/L6/L8/L9/L15/L44 all 'answer cheapest, call the sure stat'). The sameness peaks in Ch2-3. (3) Too many units are solved puzzles with fixed squads and hand-written 'aha' answers, so replay value is low. There is little variance, because the rival hand is visible and the best line is deterministic, so 'skilled 99%' units become rote after the first clear. (4) Breathers are sparse and inconsistently placed. L7, L17, L27, L37, L47 and L55 are good, but Ch5 and Ch6 stack 4+ hard units between breathers (L48-L54, L56-L58). (5) Chapter 6 has three consecutive hard chases (L56-58) before the semi, which risks a wall. (6) The finale L60 is truncated in the data, and L59 has no 3-star reward card, so the climax feels under-specified. (7) Format-weight units (L31-L40) require the player to learn three career lines per card, which is a big cognitive jump at once, with L38 and L40 especially dense. (8) Reason to return is mostly outside the content set (packs, albums, daily chase). The content gives star cards and pitch/emote unlocks, which is good, but rewards climb only mildly relative to difficulty. Fix: add 6-8 optional side-challenges or remix variants (random hands, rival re-rolls) after each boss, and soften the late difficulty ramp with hand-tuned casual win rates ≥70% on 1-star.
