Add deck-driven endless modes, a rotating daily fixture generator, rival personalities that recur with different hands, and a difficulty-assist (hint reveal after two losses) to smooth the late curve. Rewards should scale with difficulty: L56-L60 pay similar coins to L8 relative to effort, and 3rd-star card rewards of rival cards feel good but are generic.
