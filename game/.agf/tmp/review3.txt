FAIL level_design: level-design review 6.8/10 (bar 8), 3 level(s) below 6/10: The set is a coherent, well-structured 60-unit ladder. It has clear one-mechanic-per-unit teaching, good bosses and breathers, and a sensible overall difficulty ramp. Weak spots: an overly easy Ch1, a few units that don't actually teach what they claim (L5, L6, L34, L40), crowded mechanic stacking in Ch5-6, three consecutive hard chases at the end, near-duplicate 'wildcard/fog/mega pot' units, and thin late-game rewards. Fairness issues come from hidden-info (fog) units with 50% casual win rates, unverified solvability ('> search cap', trap rate '?'), and data/name errors (altered spellings, Shami T20I R0/A0 with wickets, Najibullah/Klaassen/Guptil/Hetmyer cards showing odd Economy values despite 'never bowled'). Verdict: solid mid-tier, not yet top-10 polish. Curve: Strong skeleton: each chapter introduces one mechanic alone (stances, power cards, formats, fog/wildcard/mega pot), then combines, then breathers and a boss. Win rates fall from ~89% to ~50%, a sensible ramp. Problems: (1) Ch1 is too easy and too long. L1-L3 are near-auto-wins at 85-99%, and L7 is a breather at only unit 7. (2) The mechanic count in Ch5-6 outruns player skill. Fog, wildcard and mega pot arrive together with 350+ choices at the start, and skilled win rates sit at 82-92%. Casual win rates of 50-55% against skill 0.8-0.95 AI will feel like a wall. (3) The Ch6 hard chases (L56-L58) stack three punishing units in a row with no breather. L58 has a 78% skilled win rate and is likely unwinnable for casuals. (4) Breathers are inconsistently placed: L47 is easy, but L48 then spikes to 52% casual. (5) Many 'TEACH' units are not clean. L5 is called a Super Over teach, but the Super Over is only a probable outcome. L34 and L40 are too hard for an introduction. (6) Rival difficulty rests on AI skill and deck strength rather than on new decisions. (7) Rewards are flat and linear (46 coins to 500) with a pack only every 3rd unit. Late chapters need rival-card unlocks, cosmetic milestones and star-gated chests. A 2-star/3-star replay incentive isn't tied to meta upgrades. (8) Data problems: stat errors, typos in names, and Test lines that look invented (e.g. Mohammed Shami T20I R0 with 27 wickets). Test weights, ODI weights and blank lines all read as the same 'stat swap' puzzle once seen. (9) There is no 'unique rival' identity. Most rivals are random squads with a label. Add named rival captains with personality and chapter-boss cutscenes. (10) Retention beyond 60 nodes depends on the meta layer, which is thin in this content. Needed: a hard mode with 3-star remixes, weekly cups reusing these fixtures with modifiers, and rival rematches with changed squads.
