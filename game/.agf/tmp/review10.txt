FAIL level_design: level-design review 7/10 (bar 8), 0 level(s) below 6/10: The set is well structured as a mechanic-per-unit tutorial ladder with proven fair winnability, and its pre-duel stat puzzles are readable. But it has too many same-shaped 'find the sure ball' units, a late difficulty cliff (L45-L60 casual wins 54-65%), mislabelled breathers (L44), and weak replay and variance hooks. Overall: solid and well taught, but not yet a memorable Marvel Snap-class ladder. Curve: Strong skeleton: 6 chapters, each introducing one mechanic alone (stat pick, answering, tie pot, aggressive, safe, free hit, review, powerplay, format weights, captain, fog, wildcard, mega pot), then combining it, with a breather and a boss at the end. Rewards rise steadily and Club/Pro Pack boss rewards, pitches and emotes give return reasons. Problems:

1) Early ramp is too flat. L1-L10 are all plain stat-pick, with only 'which card answers which call' changing. Several units (L2, L3, L6, L8, L9) are the same cheapest-winner / sure-tie puzzle on new rosters.
2) The difficulty cliff is steep and doesn't match the stars. Casual win rates fall from ~90% to the mid-50s by L49-L60, with L50-L60 at 54-61%. That is brutal for a 2-minute casual game. Chase and boss units (L20, L30, L56-L60) sit at 56-69% mid-skill, so many players will stall behind a wall.
3) There are too many power-card teaching units that repeat. L24/L25, L21/L22/L29 and L41/L42/L46/L48/L51/L53 are variations of 'find the sure ball'. Many 'optimal moves > search cap' units are solved by reading the coach text. The decision is closer to a puzzle than a duel, so it risks being solved once and then repeated.
4) Breather placement is uneven. L44 is labelled BREATHER but has a 75% win rate and rated 'wildcard defence' difficulty. L55 is a breather straight into the hard chases. L47 and L37 are fine.
5) Information and fairness. Pre-deal hand preview plus on-screen aids make most units deterministic puzzles, with no variance or surprise. That hurts replay and the 'stat argument' fantasy. Real-stat hands can also repeat the same legends (Salt, Surya, Kohli, Rashid) in many units, so the roster feels small.
6) Retention is thin in the unit list. There is no per-chapter reward beyond the 3rd-star card, and no replay hooks on cleared nodes such as modifiers, daily variants or star-gated chests. The 3-star criteria are often ones you reach just by following the coach text.
7) Some star goals are unclear or miscalibrated. L60's text is truncated.

Fixes: add two or three varied-pacing 'duel' nodes per chapter with fewer scripts, soften the L45+ ramp, and add replay modifiers.
