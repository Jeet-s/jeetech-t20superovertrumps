Stage 2 is done: the full game experience is built on stage 1's duel engine. `npx tsc --noEmit` has 0 errors, `npm run pitfalls` reports 0 errors and 0 warnings, and `npm run validate` shows 60/60 rivals solvable with difficulty rising from about 0.09 to 0.44. `npm run playtest` still passes; the engine itself is untouched.

I checked every screen in a temporary web preview at 360×640 and 360×740, and played real taps through a ball: pick a stat, confirm, the rival's card flips, the SIX banner fires, then the rival's call comes up. That preview has since been removed. Nothing has run on a device yet. I did not rerun the level-design review because the levels didn't change, so it still sits at stage 1's 7.3/10, below the bar of 8.

**Screens:**
- **Home (title screen):** the logo art and the mascot key art, both with idle motion, plus floodlight beams. Pills show stars, coins and packs, with chapter progress and one big "Play · Rival N". Side buttons open Daily, Online, Deck, Packs and Album; Ladder, Shop and Settings sit underneath.
- **Ladder:** the 60-rival map with chapter banners, boss nodes and stars. Tapping a rival opens a pre-duel card with its personality, rule chips, both decks, the three star goals and the reward.
- **Duel:** built to the target image.
  - Scoreboard strip with the 6-ball (or 3-ball Super Over) tracker.
  - Grass pitch with the rival's card, their deck strip (spent cards greyed) and the pot ring.
  - Your big stat card, where each row shows how many of their remaining cards it beats.
  - Flame/shield stance buttons beside the card, plus swipe up/down on it.
  - Your hand, which previews ✓/✗ when answering a call.
  - Power medallions (Free Hit, Review, Powerplay, Wildcard) and Confirm.
  - Captain picker, Review choice, Fog card back, a coach line on each rule-teaching rival, and the tutorial hand on rivals 1–2.
  - Win flow: the board celebrates, then stars pop and coins count up, with pack, card and chest chips and a Share button. Losing shows a fail popup with a coaching tip.
- **Meta screens:**
  - Packs: the reveal ceremony, an odds table, pity counters, and free packs by video (3 a day).
  - Album: 10 country pages with page rewards, and a card-detail popup with T20I/ODI/Test tabs, the stats-as-of line and the Form tier.
  - Deck builder.
  - Daily: the 7-day login streak, the Daily Chase and its leaderboard, and reminders, which only ask for permission after you opt in.
  - Shop: four fixed-price cards a day, store products, Restore purchases.
  - Settings: adds colour-blind mode, reduced motion and the privacy link.
- **Online:** Quick Duel waits up to 8 seconds for a real player, then an AI rival steps in. Room codes use an on-screen keypad (no typing). A live duel between two people runs host-authoritative through a new small rules file, `src/game/pvp.ts`, with fair matched hands and plain stats. It also has the 8 fixed emotes, handling for an opponent leaving or a lost connection, and a most-wins board.
- **Store screenshots:** six scenes — home, pick, aggressive, superover, packs, ladder. `store/FACTS.md` now lists every new system with its real numbers.

I also sliced the art sheets into single sprites (`assets/ui`, made by `scripts/dev/bake-ui.py`), mapped the 121 avatars' idle/cheer/fuming poses (`src/avatars.ts`), and added an Android Back handler.

**Gaps to know about:**
- There are no Season Track, Weekly Format Cup or Profile screens; Settings shows a short stats summary instead.
- The Remove Ads and coin rows only appear when the store returns products, so builds without IAP won't show a Remove Ads button.
- On a 360-wide phone the longest button labels (e.g. "GO AGGRO!" beside three power buttons) rely on font shrink-to-fit. That works on Android but not in the web preview.
