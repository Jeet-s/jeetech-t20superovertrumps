**Rendering and game feel** (360×640 web preview)
- **Ball-result overlay:** it now sits on its own opaque plate in fixed rows (stat label, YOU-VS-THEM cards, the two values, then "SIX!" / "+3 RUNS"). I measured the text positions every 120 ms through several balls and nothing overlaps a face, name or value.
- **Run tags:** "+3" / "+6" fly to the scoreboard. After a ball, the opponent's revealed card stays in their slot.
- **Victory popup:** the duel screen is hidden behind it, so the dimmed board, the cut-off opponent card and the empty dashed bar are gone. It has a trophy, stars that pop in turn, confetti, a coin count-up and a "Watch a video: double to N" button.
- **Tutorial hand:** every spot on the six stat rows covered some text, which is where "Economy · lowe" came from. The hand now only points at PLAY. The recommended row marks itself with the BEST tag, a pulsing outline and a ring from its stat chip.
- **"RIVAL 1" / "OVER 2" banners:** they now have their own framed plate over a deeper dim instead of drawing straight over the card's text.
- **Packs badge:** "OPEN" shows in full on the small-phone layout.
