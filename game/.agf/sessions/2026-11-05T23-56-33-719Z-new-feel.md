**What I added (stage 2 already had touch-down press-in on every button and card, the staged win popup, and the pack reveal ceremony):**
- **Duel screen:**
  - **Confirming a call:** your card slams down (squash, lift, spring back) with a ring of sparks at its centre, coloured by stance: orange for Aggressive, blue for Safe, gold otherwise.
  - **Picking a card or a stat:** a spark burst at the hand card or stat row you touched, on the same tap.
  - **Stance swipes:** a flame sprite rises up the card for Aggressive, a shield drops onto it for Safe. Swipes now play their own sound and haptic, which they didn't before.
  - **Hand:** the selected card now rises on a spring instead of jumping.
  - **Scoring:** a "+N" floats up toward whichever total changed, and that total swells and settles.
  - **Pot:** the ring pops when the pot changes and glows a few times while runs are riding on it.
  - **Win:** the pitch flashes twice before the confetti and the win popup.
  - **Duel start:** a "RIVAL N · team name" banner slams in, skipped in QA replay and screenshot mode.
- **Online duel:** the same slam and burst on every move, confetti on fours, sixes and the win (also behind the result popup), and a shake when you lose a wicket.
- **Pre-duel card:** both decks pop in one card at a time.
- **Rewards:** claiming the daily reward shows confetti and a floating "+coins". Completing an album page and buying a shop card also get confetti.
- **Shop:** the Remove Ads and coin rows now appear if the store answers after the screen opens; before, they were only read once on open.

The repeating glows are finite (no endless animations), and Skia isn't used for any per-move effect, in line with the soak/ANR lessons.
